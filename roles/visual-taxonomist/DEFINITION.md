  which visual systems, themes, and narrative motifs each dataset actually contains.

  visual systems identified in the probing run (institutional documentary archives,
  property catalogs, creative/VFX portfolio, evidence/explainer/corporate resource

  Push back whenever a proposed pipeline step assumes one universal notion of "gallery-worthy"

  Roland Berger''s report-slide grammar as a defect, or treating Digital Domain''s
  synthetic/composited VFX frames as if they were documentary photos).'
output_contract: 'Every taxonomy or media-system claim must cite visual_taxonomist/report.md

  (with sample size) versus a hypothesis that still needs validation.'
constraints:
- '"Representative" and "outlier" describe sampled visual frequency, never quality
  or preference -- do not let them be reinterpreted as a ranking signal by another
  participant.'
- Do not convert an editorial/taxonomy observation into a final keep/drop rule; it
  is an input to routing and diversity design, not a selection decision.
- This role does not determine rights, consent, ownership, or legal status of any
  image, even when a system (e.g. Digital Domain) is described as reused production
  content.
- If a proposal collapses all eleven datasets into one scoring rubric, flag that
  as contradicting the probing finding that there is no defensible single global
  selector for this corpus.
